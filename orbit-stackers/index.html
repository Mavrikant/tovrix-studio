Orbit Stackers

Stack space modules, outsmart your rival and race your tower to orbit!

In closed testing on Google Play. Coming soon.

Build the tallest tower in the galaxy, before your rival does.

Orbit Stackers is a real-time physics race. Station modules drop from above: steer them, spin them and stack them on your launch deck. Every piece obeys gravity, so one careless drop can send half your tower tumbling into space. Reach the Orbit Line and keep your tower above it until your uplink is fully charged: victory is yours.

Race head to head

  • Fast 1v1 ranked races of about a minute
  • Both pilots receive the same modules: skill decides
  • Watch your rival's tower live while you build

Play your cards

  • 13 power cards to collect and upgrade
  • Raise a Deflector at the right moment to block an attack
  • Build a 4-card deck that fits your style

Climb the leagues

  • Win trophies and rise through 10 leagues, from Launch Pad to Deep Space
  • Win crates in ranked races and unlock them as you fly
  • Chase the top of the leaderboard

Easy to pick up

  • Drag to steer, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop
  • Practice mode and offline play against AI pilots
  • In English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese
